Read this before you judge the numbers
This is a model record. Every qualifying pick is graded at the price it was posted (no slippage assumed) and settled automatically — wins, losses, pushes and all. A 96-pick sample over 19 days is enough to be transparent, not enough to be certain.
Read each market on its own. Moneyline, spread and totals are set by different desks and have diverged in this sample. A single blended ROI can average away a segment that is losing money, so the headline is split by market and the full breakdown is in the table above. No pick is ever removed or re-priced after the fact — when the rules change, the record is segmented by date instead.
